Welcome to Vorqexa

Vorqexa is a transparent, decentralised perpetual DEX built around clarity, control, and a focused trading experience.

These docs explain what Vorqexa is, how the trading flow works, what infrastructure supports the platform, and what to review before trading.

We aim to keep the product straightforward and the information around it clear, including the role of the technology that supports the trading experience.

1. What Is Vorqexa?

Vorqexa is a user-facing perpetual trading platform for supported markets. It brings market information, order placement, position management, and account tools into one trading experience.

Vorqexa focuses on a clear trading experience around:

  • Market discovery
  • Orderbook-based trading
  • Trading account setup
  • Order and position management
  • Portfolio and PnL information
  • Wallet connectivity
  • A focused, understandable interface

2. How Vorqexa Works

Start Trading

Getting started takes a few setup steps. Connecting a wallet is only the beginning; your trading account must be created, trading enabled, and the account funded before you can place a trade.

1. Connect Your Wallet

Connect a compatible wallet to the Vorqexa trading application.

2. Create Your Trading Account

After your wallet is connected, create your trading account to access the trading features.

3. Enable Trading

Complete the required setup and enable trading for your account.

4. Deposit Funds

Deposit supported assets into your trading account before placing an order.

5. Choose a Market

Select a supported perpetual market and review its price information and orderbook.

6. Place an Order

Choose an available order type, set your parameters, review the order, and submit it.

7. Execution

Once submitted, your order is processed by the trading infrastructure supporting Vorqexa and handled according to the applicable market rules.

8. Manage Your Position

After execution, monitor your position, margin, PnL, and other available trading information through Vorqexa.

The flow: Connect Wallet → Create Account → Enable Trading → Deposit → Choose Market → Place Order → Execute → Manage Position

3. Trading

Orderbook Trading

Vorqexa provides an orderbook-based trading experience. The orderbook displays available buy and sell interest for supported markets and gives traders a clear view of the market before submitting an order.

Orders

The order types available on Vorqexa depend on the current trading configuration. They may include market, limit, Immediate-or-Cancel (IOC), Fill-or-Kill (FOK), Post-Only, and Reduce-Only orders.

Order availability can change. Always check the live trading interface before submitting an order.

Positions & PnL

After an order is executed, your position can be monitored through the trading interface. Depending on the market and current platform functionality, available information may include position size, entry price, mark price, unrealized PnL, realized PnL, margin, leverage, and liquidation information.

4. Built-in Trading Journal

What It Is

Vorqexa includes a trading journal built directly into the app, so you can keep a record of your trading and review how you trade over time.

Log your trades, track your emotional state alongside them, and review your PnL history, all inside the app.

The journal is for your own review and does not constitute financial advice.

5. Accounts & Deposits

Trading Account

Depositing

Before trading, deposit supported assets into your trading account. The available assets, networks, and deposit process can change as the platform develops.

  • The supported network
  • The destination
  • Any applicable transaction or network fees

6. Perpetual Trading

What Are Perpetuals?

Perpetual contracts are derivative instruments that allow traders to take long or short positions without a traditional expiration date.

Perpetual trading can involve leverage. Leverage can increase the size of potential gains, but it can also increase losses and the risk of liquidation.

Before trading, make sure you understand margin, leverage, liquidation, funding, slippage, fees, and market volatility.

7. Fees

Trading costs depend on the market and the current platform configuration. Possible costs include trading fees, funding payments, network fees, and other applicable transaction costs.

Always review the current fee information shown by Vorqexa before trading. Do not rely on outdated documentation or third-party fee information.

8. Security & Wallet Safety

Protect Your Wallet

Vorqexa uses compatible wallets to give users access to the trading application. Your wallet is an important part of your security, so always verify that you are using the official Vorqexa website before connecting.

Never share your seed phrase, private key, recovery phrase, or wallet password. Vorqexa will never ask you for these credentials.

Before approving a transaction or signing a message, read the request carefully and make sure you understand what your wallet is asking you to approve.

Trading Risk

Perpetual trading involves substantial risk. Market volatility, leverage, liquidation, funding costs, slippage, wallet security, smart-contract risk, network risk, and infrastructure dependencies can all affect your trading experience.

10. Infrastructure

How the Platform Is Supported

Vorqexa is the product and user-facing trading experience. To support core perpetual trading functionality, Vorqexa uses established third-party trading infrastructure.

For transparency, core trading services are provided by that third-party infrastructure rather than built and operated by Vorqexa itself.

This allows the Vorqexa team to focus on building the product, interface, user experience, brand, and community while relying on established infrastructure for core trading services.

What This Means for Users

You interact with Vorqexa as the trading platform. The underlying infrastructure operates behind the scenes to support the trading functionality you use.

We distinguish Vorqexa’s product and user experience from the underlying third-party infrastructure that supports trading.

12. Glossary

Orderbook

A collection of active buy and sell orders for a market.

Perpetual

A derivative contract designed to allow traders to maintain positions without a traditional expiration date.

Margin

Collateral used to support a trading position.

Leverage

The use of collateral to control a larger position. Leverage can amplify both gains and losses.

Liquidation

The forced closing of a position when the required margin is no longer maintained.

Funding

Periodic payments between positions in a perpetual market that help keep the contract price aligned with the underlying market.

PnL

Profit and Loss, representing the financial result of a trading position.

Slippage

The difference between the expected execution price and the actual execution price.
